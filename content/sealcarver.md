title: Sealcarver
---

Every Codex casting begins as lines on a surface. This is a practice slate: choose what sits in the Heart, set the Daggers around it, close the Ring, and watch the seal take shape. Carve enough of them and you may find your hand has drawn something that already has a name.

The grammar itself is taught on the [[codex-magic|Codex Magic]] page.
