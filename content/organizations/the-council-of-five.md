**Summary**: The **Council of Five**, the five hereditary offices that govern [[andaluria|Andaluria's]] reconstruction from the Plaza del Sol in Jesaña. Funded by [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]], its officials are the stewards charged with rebuilding the holy capital after the Crisis.

---

## Overview

After [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim's]] fall drove [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] from the region, ruined [[andaluria|Andaluria]] was left to govern itself, and the Council of Five rose to the task: five hereditary offices, seated at Jesaña's Plaza del Sol, charged with rebuilding [[hilltop|Hilltop's]] holy capital and keeping its people fed and its trade moving. The Holy See funds their work from the taxes it gathers on the merchant-roads at the continent's centre. To Andaluria, they are the honest stewards of a wounded land.

## Notable Members

The five offices are: **Hellenor Galdez**, Harbormaster (ships and trade); **Marwin Chopex**, Quartermaster (storehouses, armories, and supply); **Andrea Nocta**, Captain of the Nightwatch (guards, patrols, and watch); **Miquela Shaas**, Archivist (records, decrees, and histories); and **Exel Legasi**, Market Keeper (guilds, fairs, and coinage).
