title: The Five Heroes
---

**Summary**: The five heroes who fought and brought down [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333-334 VR, the event that reshaped Althas's politics and its faith: the Voldaen High Prince [[edrion-voldis|Edrion Voldis]], [[cassio|Saint Cassio]] of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]], [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]], [[lael|Lael the Reborn]], and [[lyra|Archmage Lyra]] of [[polaris|Polaris]].

---

## The battle

In 333 VR the [[the-ophanim|Ophanim]] landed at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] and devastated it, forcing [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] to relocate to central Althas. The following year, 334 VR, the Five Heroes brought the Ophanim down. Its death shook the faith and Althas itself to their roots. The land around its corpse is still permanently, unnaturally dark.

## The heroes

- **[[edrion-voldis|Edrion Voldis]]**, High Prince of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] and father of Queen [[valis-voldis|Valis Voldis]], died in the fighting, setting off the Voldis Succession Crisis.
- **[[cassio|Saint Cassio]]** of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]], a scholar of the age, also died bringing the Ophanim down.
- **[[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]]**, baseborn half-brother of Edrion, survived: honored by the soldiery and the smallfolk but spurned by the capital's oldest houses, he became the champion of the common people and founded the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen Republic]].
- **[[lael|Lael]], called the Reborn**, fell in the fighting and was returned to life by his [[miracles|Miracle of Rebirth]], which keeps his body from ever truly dying. He is remembered as the hero who could not stay dead.
- **[[lyra|Archmage Lyra]]** of [[polaris|Polaris]], sister to [[cassio|Saint Cassio]] and a member of the Triumvirate of Polaris, survived and set the whole account down in her book *Verba Ophanim*, from the Ophanim's arrival at Hilltop to its dying words. She is seldom seen in public now; rumor holds that her [[the-ophanim|OphDreams]] and her brother's death have worn her down with grief over the years.
