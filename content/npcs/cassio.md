---

Saint Cassio was a scholar of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] and one of the [[the-five-heroes|Five Heroes]] who fought [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333-334 VR. He died in the fighting, alongside the Voldaen High Prince [[edrion-voldis|Edrion Voldis]]. His sister, [[lyra|Archmage Lyra]] of [[polaris|Polaris]], survived and recorded the Ophanim's story in *Verba Ophanim*.
