Archmage Lyra is one of the [[the-five-heroes|Five Heroes]] who fought and brought down [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333-334 VR. A member of the Triumvirate of Polaris (see [[polaris|Polaris]]) and sister to [[cassio|Saint Cassio]], who died in that same battle, she survived and set the whole account down in her book *Verba Ophanim*. In the years since she has withdrawn from public life.
