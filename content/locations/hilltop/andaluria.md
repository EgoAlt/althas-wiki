**Summary**: Andaluria, the capital region of [[hilltop|Hilltop]] around [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim's]] corpse. A wine country rebuilt after the Crisis under its Council of Five, where of late the nights have grown unnaturally long, the moon lingers overhead, and monsters prowl the fields, a lived-in land sliding toward an eternal dark, with hunters rising to hold it back.

---

## Overview

### The nights grow long

Of late, something has gone wrong with Andaluria's nights. They lengthen with each sunset, the moon lingers overhead where it should set, and monsters prowl the fields where revelers once danced. The old defenses, the folk rites and the Church's sacred rites alike, have lost their bite. People whisper that death has come to Andaluria, and fear what the coming Midsummer Festival will bring. The wealthy hide from the horror in masquerades at the hilltop castillo of the Condesa [[ysabela-delamona|Ysabela Delamoña IV]], while ordinary folk fend for themselves and the Sombral Spade takes up the hunt where lord and church will not.

### Governed and rebuilt

Andaluria is governed by its [[the-council-of-five|Council of Five]], seated at Jesaña's Plaza del Sol, and rebuilt on the taxes [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] gathers from the merchant-roads at the continent's centre, in begrudging trade with the now-independent [[armada|Armada]] to the south. Wine remains its lifeblood and its chief export.

## History

### Wine country at the heart of Hilltop

Since [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]], Andaluria was the seat of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] and the heart of [[hilltop|Hilltop]]: sirocco winds, volcanic soil, and the vineyards of the Serracorte, their wine carried down the Valmorria to the capital, Jesaña, on the southern coast. Then [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] fell here in 333 VR. Its landing devastated the region and drove the Holy See and most of its refugees to central Althas, but Andaluria survived, and over the thirty years since has rebuilt itself, its festivals and harvests and revelry returning to the dark volcanic hills.
