**Summary**: **The Lodestar Pact**, the treaty of 167 VR by which the scholars and mages of the north broke away from [[voldaen|Voldaen]] to found [[polaris|Polaris]], and by which Voldaen, Polaris, and [[hilltop|Hilltop]] bound themselves to jointly colonize the Witherwild, settled peacefully rather than by the war that had scarred the continent half a century earlier.

---

Half a century after [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]], the northern academies and their mages, a cadre of codexcasters forged into a political power during that civil war, had grown too great to sit easily under the crown of [[house-voldis|House Voldis]]. Rather than repeat the bloodshed so recently ended, [[valeran-voldis|Valeran]]'s heir King [[castus-voldis|Castus]] and the north settled their split by negotiated partition, ratified by the Pontiff of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] on the fiftieth anniversary of the Blessed King's victory. Out of it came [[polaris|Polaris]], an independent magocracy ruled by a Triumvirate of Archmages, the lodestar of the north that the Pact takes its name from. The same treaty also bound Voldaen, Polaris, and [[hilltop|Hilltop]] to a shared venture: the joint colonization of the untouched Witherwild. As part of the settlement, King Castus's younger brother, the prince Castorius, renounced his own claim and afterward passed out of the record.
